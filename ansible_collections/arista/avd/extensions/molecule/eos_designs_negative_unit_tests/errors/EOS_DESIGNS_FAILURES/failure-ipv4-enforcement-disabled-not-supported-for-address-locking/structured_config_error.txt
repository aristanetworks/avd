Remove `address_locking_settings.locked_address.ipv4_enforcement_disabled: true`. Instead, configure `address_locking_settings.dhcp_servers_ipv4` with `address_locking_settings.local_interface`, or configure `address_locking_settings.dhcp_server_interfaces` and set `avd_design_future.fix_address_locking_dhcp_server_interfaces: true`.
