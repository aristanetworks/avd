# Porting Ansible Playbooks from `eos_config_deploy_cvp` to `cv_deploy`

Welcome to the porting guide for transitioning from the `eos_config_deploy_cvp` role to the modern `cv_deploy` role within the Arista AVD ecosystem. This migration marks a key evolution in network automation strategy, moving from pushing configurations directly to individual devices toward a more robust, centralized model managed by Arista CloudVision. By leveraging cv_deploy, you unlock the full power of CloudVision's features, including comprehensive change control, configuration compliance, and a full audit trail. This guide provides a clear, step-by-step process for updating your Ansible inventory and playbooks to ensure a smooth and successful transition.
